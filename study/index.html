10-Minute Study Trainer

A 10-minute daily routine: read faster at a pace you can still understand, then keep what you read.

Today's 10 minutes

  1. Review (~4 min). 0 cards due. Answer from memory before revealing.
  2. Reading drill (~4 min). Paste ~1 page of today's training material, read with the pacer, then write down what you remember.
  3. Make 3–5 cards (~2 min). Turn the key points you missed or must not forget into questions.

Streak: 0 days. Review comes first: recalling yesterday's material before adding new material is what makes it stick.

Why this routine

  • Retrieval practice: trying to recall something strengthens memory far more than re-reading it. That's why you type an answer before you reveal it.
  • Spaced repetition: each card comes back after 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, then 32 days, just before you'd forget it. If you forget a card, it starts over.
  • Pacer: a moving highlight cuts down on re-reading and drifting attention. Comprehension sets your speed: the target pace only goes up when you recall at least 70%.
  • Good cards are small and specific: "Who approves refunds over $500?" works better than "Explain the refund policy". For processes, make one card per step: "After X, what's next?"
